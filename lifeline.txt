Attack Lifecycle
 
Sl. no
Step
What the attacker is doing
Example 
How to spot or stop it
1
Recon
Researching the target
Looking up staff emails, scanning websites
Patch public systems, limit public info
2
Initial Access
Getting in the door
Phishing email, stolen password
Email filtering, MFA
3
Execution
Running their code
Opening a malicious attachment launches PowerShell
Alert when Word starts PowerShell
4
Persistence
Making sure they can come back
New scheduled task or startup entry
Check startup items and new tasks
5
Privilege Escalation
Getting admin rights
Exploiting a bug to become SYSTEM
Patch, give users least privilege
6
Défense Evasion
Hiding
Renaming malware as svchost.exe, clearing logs
Watch for odd names and cleared logs
7
Credential Access
Stealing passwords
Dumping memory of the LSASS process
Credential Guard, alert on LSASS access
8
Discovery and Lateral Movement
Exploring, then spreading
Running whoami, net, then logging into other PCs
Segment the network, watch for one account on many PCs
9
Command and Control (C2)
Remote control
Malware "calls home" every minute
Look for regular repeated connections
10
Goal: Exfiltration or Impact
Taking data or causing damage
Uploading files, then encrypting everything (ransomware)
Block large uploads, keep offline backups
