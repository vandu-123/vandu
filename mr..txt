### Case Study: The Missing Research Files

Case Number: DF/2026/047 Organisation: NexaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru Incident Date: 28 October 2026 Nature: Suspected unauthorised copying of confidential data.

### 1\. Background

NexaTech Solutions suspected that confidential company documents were copied from an employee's Windows 11 laptop to an external USB device without authorisation. A digital forensic investigation was conducted to examine the laptop and USB drive.

### 2\. Evidence Received

* E-01: Dell laptop running Windows 11.

* E-02: SanDisk 16 GB USB drive.

* E-03: Laptop forensic image.

* E-04: USB forensic image.

### 3\. Investigation Findings

A. Windows Registry Analysis

* SanDisk USB device was identified in the USBSTOR registry.

* Device information was associated with the Windows user profile Rahul.

B. Laptop File Analysis

Five files were identified, including:

* Product_Design.pdf

* Project_Blueprint.docx

* Financial_Report.xlsx

* Employee_Details.xlsx

* Project_Summary.txt

C. USB Analysis

Three matching filenames were found in the USB image:

* Product_Design.pdf

* Project_Blueprint.docx

* Financial_Report.xlsx

### 4\. Timeline

* 10:15 AM – USB connected.

* 10:22 AM – Research documents accessed.

* 10:28 AM – Three documents identified in USB image.

* 11:05 AM – USB disconnected.

### 5\. Conclusion

The investigation identified a SanDisk USB device connected to the employee's workstation and three company document filenames present in the USB forensic image. These findings indicate possible data transfer. However, without hash comparison and further verification, it cannot be conclusively established that the files are identical or that unauthorised copying occurred. The available evidence also does not independently prove who performed the transfer.
